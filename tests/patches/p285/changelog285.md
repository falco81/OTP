## 1.0.285 — 15 November 2026

Added
- **Signing in with a certificate: when the card is taken out** (`admin_cert_card`: `off` - the default, nothing changes and nginx is written as before; `signout`; `grace`, with `admin_cert_card_s` 5-300 s, default 15, and `admin_cert_card_grace_s` 5-600 s, default 30) - as the kit *card-removal-watch*: the page loads a picture from the certificate's port, a new TLS handshake each time that only the card can sign (nginx keeps no TLS session and no connection there while it is on); 1 px - the card is in, 2 px - out and the session waits, 403 - over. `signout` ends the session at the first check without the card; `grace` covers the page with a countdown (*Put the card back*, the seconds left in a ring that empties, *Sign out*) and the server answers nothing of the session until it is back; another card ends it at once; the sign-in page says why and tries nothing by itself. The state is kept with the session in the database (either node of a cluster, a restart forgets nothing); the server ends a watched session whose page stopped checking; every end is in the events. Kind to the card: one check at a time, one for all the tabs of a browser, a pause after a check that waited on a window of the card's, a lost connection asked again before it counts. The page's policy lets the check's picture in only for a watched session. A password, Windows and RADIUS are not touched.
