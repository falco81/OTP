## 1.0.324 — 23 November 2026

**Security (RADIUS)**: a realm switched off sent its people to the realm that takes everything else (`*`) - signed in by that realm's way, a code alone where their own asked for the directory's password too. A realm switched off is now as a client's range switched off: its names (and its other suffixes) are not answered under a wider one.

**Bug fix (load balancer)**: with more than 64 networks of balancers (a CDN's ranges), root's helper kept the first 64 without a word - each balancer past them was taken for the client, its clients passed the address lists as it and shared one count of sign-ins. All of them are put in, 256 at most - more is refused when saved; the field takes them (it held some 40 before, 500 letters).

**Bug fix**: the hourly look for the names people go by (from the directory) looked at the same first 100 tokens every hour when no directory knew them - the tokens after them were never filled. It goes on from where it stopped.
