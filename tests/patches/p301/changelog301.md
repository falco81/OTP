## 1.0.301 — 17 November 2026

**SELinux on a pane of its own** - *Settings → SELinux*, right under *High security*, as the kit (and acme) has it.

- The card as the kit draws it: a light for each node beside the title (enforcing green, permissive yellow, an error red; turning while a node does what was asked), *SELinux is each machine's own* in a cluster, and **"SELinux refused nothing on this node"** in green when nothing was refused - *What SELinux refuses* is always there on a node where SELinux is on.
- **The upgrade band: every node on a row of its own**, one under the other, as acme draws it - its name (the one whose turn it is in the colour of a link), its step, how far, what happened, when; above them how far as a whole, whose turn it is and how many nodes are done.
- Every link to it goes there: the security check (*High security*), the alert, what is to be done by hand; their words say *Settings → SELinux*. *High security* keeps its own check and settings.
